// 02 · story

开篇故事 — 三段式：没有手 → 时机 → 缺的那只手

English

Your AI colleague is brilliant — and completely handless.

It reads your code, browses your docs, writes your tests. But walk it into a workshop, a clinic, a lab, or a barn, and it goes blind: it can't feel the bearing wear in your compressor, can't read the sterilizer's cycle counter, can't tell you the tractor threw a code at 3 a.m.

Anthropic's new Model Hardware Standard just gave agents a standard way to operate physical devices — standard drivers, plain-language safety labels, safe read/write calls. The first adopters are top labs and advanced manufacturers, where Claude now tunes instruments overnight.

What's still missing is the box on your side: something that speaks fieldbus to your equipment and MHS to your agent, watches the night shift, keeps your data physically yours, and puts permission on physical buttons you can actually press.

That's the Deck. One device, six jobs — a desk your AI coworker sits at.

中文

你的 AI 同事很聪明 —— 但它没有手。

它读你的代码、翻你的文档、写你的测试。可一旦走进车间、诊所、实验室或谷仓，它就瞎了：它摸不到空压机轴承的磨损，读不到灭菌器的循环计数，更不知道拖拉机凌晨三点报了码。

Anthropic 新发布的模型硬件标准（MHS）刚给 Agent 开了一条操作物理设备的标准通道——标准化驱动、自然语言安全标签、安全的读写指令。首批接入的是顶级实验室和先进制造商：Claude 已经能在那里通宵调仪器了。

还缺的是你这一侧的那只手：一台对下说现场总线、对上说 MHS 的设备；一台替你值守夜班、把数据物理地留在你手里、把权限放在真实按键上的设备。

这就是 Deck。一台设备，六件事 —— 你的 AI 同事坐镇的桌面。

设计说明：此节纯文字 + 一张 hero 延伸图，不加参数表。故事先于规格，Kickstarter 高转化页面第一节从不说"参数"。叙事钩子按顺序回答：为什么现在（MHS 时机）→ 为什么是我（缺的那只手）。

English · 北向消息网关（本节下半屏）

Northbound: the message gateway. Southbound speaks fieldbus to machines; northbound speaks MQTT to everything else — the same capability class Moxa's AIG series and USR's edge gateways ship, minus the cloud lock-in:

  • Built-in MQTT broker (3.1.1 / 5.0) — every bridged device mirrors onto a topic tree deck/{device}/{register}; telemetry publishes itself. Node-RED · Home Assistant · Grafana · SCADA subscribe instead of polling.

  • Cloud bridge (opt-in) — TLS-bridged to your own broker: Azure IoT · AWS IoT · Alibaba Cloud · EMQX · self-hosted. Off by default, labeled when on.

// 05 · privacy · 核心差异节

隐私架构 — 本项目的灵魂卖点

English

The floppy's form. The vault's soul.

The Deck's archive lives on a removable, hardware-encrypted cartridge — up to 512 GB. The key lives in the cartridge, not the machine. Pull it out and your history physically leaves with you: nothing readable stays behind.

And your private data is computed by a local model, on-device. Telemetry, logs, histories — inference happens where the data lives, so understanding your data never requires uploading it.

Compute and storage are cleanly split:

compute plane:  local model · NPU anomaly detection · MHS registry · protocol bridge   (always on)
storage plane:  hardware-encrypted cartridge · 8 GB eMMC buffer                         (swappable · yours)
private data:   computed by the local model, on the Deck                                (never uploaded by default)
cartridge out:  compute keeps running → eMMC buffer · history rides in your pocket

What the split buys you:

  • Privacy by physics — pull the cartridge and it's yours; no remote wipe needed, no "trust us"
  • Local-model privacy — personal data computed on the Deck itself
  • Independent upgrade — swap cartridge capacity without touching compute
  • Failure isolation — a dead cartridge doesn't kill the sensor
  • Audit path — hand the auditor the cartridge, not the machine
中文

软盘的形态，保险库的内核。

Deck 的档案库装在一张可拔的硬件加密存储卡里——最高 512GB。密钥在卡里，不在机器里。拔出来，你的历史就物理地跟你走了：机器里不留任何可读内容。

你的隐私数据由本地模型计算，就在设备上。遥测、日志、历史——推理发生在数据所在之处，理解你的数据从不需要上传它。

计算与存储，干净分离：拔卡不停机——计算继续，缓存在 eMMC；换卡不碰固件；卡坏不影响传感；审计交卡不交机器。

排布：本节是全页视觉重心——左文右图，架构图用等宽字体渲染（非位图），保持与整页一致的"工程感"。"Privacy by physics" 是本节金句，社交传播时单独做成金句卡。
// 06 · risk prediction

风险预测 — 从"预测维护"升级为"三类风险"

English

Risk, briefed — not buried.

Most "predictive maintenance" boxes predict one thing: failure. Real equipment carries three risks, and the Deck briefs all of them, every morning, per fleet:

  • Failure risk — RUL curves: bearing wear, belt stretch, filter clog, refrigerant loss. Alerts days or weeks before, never after.
  • Safety risk — CO trends, thermal-runaway precursors, overload patterns. Escalated before danger, not logged after it.
  • Compliance risk — inspection deadlines, sensor-expiry dates. The boring one that costs the most when missed.

Each brief carries four fields: likelihood · time window · recommended action · evidence. The agent doesn't just watch — it reads the brief and proposes the next step. You approve it on the physical button.

中文

风险要简报，不是埋起来。多数"预测维护"盒子只会预测一件事：故障。真实世界的设备背着三类风险，Deck 每天早上按车队逐台简报：故障风险（RUL 曲线：轴承磨损、皮带拉伸、滤芯堵塞、制冷剂流失）· 安全风险（CO 趋势、热失控前兆、过载模式——危险之前升级预警）· 合规风险（年检与传感器到期日程——最枯燥、误期最贵的那类）。

每份简报四要素：可能性 · 时间窗 · 建议动作 · 证据。Agent 不只是看——它读简报、提方案，你在实体按键上批准。

配图建议：用落地页模拟屏截图（RISK brief · failure 4% · compliance due 12d）——已实现的 UI 比抽象插画可信。本节向医疗/诊所档位（$899）输送价值。
// 07 · night shift

夜班场景 — 情绪节：安静的守望者

English

It runs the night shift.

Your computer goes to sleep. The Deck doesn't. While you're away it keeps polling, sensing, storing — the local model watching every curve, the vault recording every cycle. Come back, and the agent greets you with what happened while you were gone: "Bearing temperature drifted 3 °C at 02:40. Risk brief filed. Nothing needs you yet."

your computer on:    OpenWorker ↔ Deck ↔ equipment   (live: agent reads, you approve)
your computer off:   Deck → senses → stores          (night shift: local model watches, vault records)
cartridge pulled:   compute continues → eMMC        (history rides in your pocket)
you return:         Deck → buffered history → OpenWorker   ("while you were away...")
中文

它值守夜班。你的电脑睡了，Deck 不睡。你不在时它持续轮询、感知、存储——本地模型盯着每条曲线，加密卡记录每个循环。你回来，Agent 迎上来："你不在的这段时间，轴承温度在 02:40 漂了 3 °C，风险简报已归档，暂时不需要你。"

主文案 — 产品故事

English · body copy

The last time your data had a shape you could hold, it lived on a floppy. You could feel its weight in your pocket, and when you left the office, it left with you. Then the cloud ate that. Your files became someone else's uptime.

The Privacy Cartridge gives the shape back: a 3.5″ floppy silhouette in armored metal — metal shutter, engraved lock icon, a violet status edge that breathes when the disk is healthy. Inside, up to 512 GB of hardware-encrypted storage. The key lives in the disk, not in the machine. Insert it in any Deck and the archive unlocks; pull it out and the slot is just an empty slot holding nothing.

Compute here. Store there. The Deck runs two planes, and they're separated on purpose. The compute plane — NPU, local model, sensing — never leaves the device. The storage plane is the cartridge. Pull it and the archive physically leaves with you; the Deck keeps running on 8 GB of internal buffer and syncs the moment you re-insert. Nothing breaks. Nothing leaks. That's what compute-storage separation means when it's made of metal instead of policy documents.

Pull-to-own: three seconds, no menus. No settings screen, no export wizard, no "preparing your archive" progress bar. You eject your data the way you ejected floppies — by hand. Your AI coworker's entire memory of your workshop fits in a shirt pocket.

中文 · 主文案

你的数据最后一次有形状、拿得起来，是它还在软盘里的时候。你能摸到它的重量，你下班走人，它跟你走。后来云把它吃掉了——你的文件变成了别人机房的在线率。

隐私卡把这个形状还给你：3.5″ 软盘轮廓、装甲金属内核——金属滑盖、铭刻锁孔、一条健康时缓慢呼吸的紫色状态边。里面是最大 512 GB 的硬件加密存储。密钥在盘里，不在机器里。插进任何一台 Deck，档案解锁；拔出来，卡槽就只是一个空槽，什么都留不下。

计算在这，存储在那。Deck 是两个平面，刻意分开。计算平面——NPU、本地模型、传感——永不离机。存储平面就是这张卡。拔卡，档案物理地跟你走；Deck 继续跑在 8 GB 内置缓冲上，回插瞬间同步。什么都不坏，什么都不漏。这就是"计算存储分离"做成金属而不是做成隐私政策的样子。

拔盘即拥有：三秒，无菜单。没有设置页，没有导出向导，没有"正在准备您的档案"进度条。你像当年退软盘一样，用手把数据退出来。你的 AI 同事对你车间的全部记忆，装得进衬衫口袋。

主文案 — 产品故事

English · body copy

Most of the machines worth watching were never built to be watched. Your washer has no RS-485. Your water heater speaks no CAN. The compressor in the back room predates BLE by twenty years. They share exactly one interface with the modern world: the plug.

The Socket Edition is the zero-wiring front door to the Deck ecosystem. Plug it into the wall. Plug the appliance into it. Done — no electrician, no protocol config, no opening the cabinet. The violet status ring breathes when it's watching; the waveform strip shows live current in fluorescent green, because watching should be visible.

What a current signature knows. Built-in sensing reads the power signature directly: start-current spikes, duty-cycle drift, running-current creep. Compressor stall. Heating-element degradation. Bearing wear in the drum motor, weeks before it's audible. The local model turns the waveform into risk classes on the Deck — and the socket registers itself on MHS like any other device, so your agent sees "washer" on the topic tree, not "unknown plug 3".

The cheapest seat in the ecosystem. Not every machine deserves a DIN-rail gateway. Some machines just need someone to notice when they start limping — that's the whole job, and it's $43 an outlet.

中文 · 主文案

值得盯的机器，大多生来就没打算被盯。你的洗衣机没有 RS-485，热水器不会说 CAN，后屋那台空压机比 BLE 老二十岁。它们与现代世界共享的接口只有一个：插头。

插座版是 Deck 生态的零接线大门。插上墙。电器插它上面。完事——不用电工，不用配协议，不用开柜子。紫环状态灯在盯的时候会呼吸；电流波形条用荧光绿显示实时电流——被盯这件事，应该看得见。

电流特征知道什么。内置传感直读功率特征：启动电流尖峰、占空比漂移、运行电流爬升。空压机堵转。加热元件老化。滚筒电机轴承磨损——在听得见之前的几周。本地模型在 Deck 上把波形变成风险等级——同时插座像任何设备一样在 MHS 注册，你的 Agent 在主题树上看到的是"洗衣机"，不是"未知插头 3"。

生态里最便宜的座位。不是每台机器都配得上一台 DIN 轨网关。有些机器只需要有人发现它开始瘸了——这就是全部工作，折合每个插座 43 美元。

风险与挑战 — 诚实的风险登记表

English

This is a hardware campaign — here's our honest risk register.

  • Supply chain. The SoC and the encrypted-cartridge controller come from two vendors; alternates are qualified for both.
  • Certification. CE/FCC in the DVT window. Our medical-adjacent positioning is monitoring only by design — we make no FDA-regulated claims and never will.
  • Spec evolution. MHS is a research preview. The Deck follows its read/write pattern and ships a compatibility layer; firmware is open-source, so spec changes land as updates, not as bricks.
  • Physical intuition limits. Like Anthropic, we acknowledge that models have limited spatial/physical reasoning — which is exactly why writes sit behind physical Approve/Deny buttons.
  • Schedule. Hardware slips. Our mitigation is scope discipline (six jobs, no more) and the monthly build log — you'll know a slip the same week we do.
  • Trademark & license. OpenWorker is an independent open-source project (MIT, © 2024 Andrew Ng). The Deck is a community companion: not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Andrew Ng, deeplearning.ai or the OpenWorker project — the name is used solely to indicate compatibility, with attribution and no code carried over. The independent-project disclaimer sits in the page hero, README top and footer.
  • Claims discipline. Detection scenarios are design goals, not guarantees; clinic scenarios describe maintenance-record storage, never patient data; safety signals are flagged for human review, never "prevented". Prices shown are campaign prices — future retail pricing is not locked.
中文

这是硬件众筹——这是我们的诚实风险清单：供应链（双供应商已验证替代）；认证（DVT 窗口送检 CE/FCC，医疗场景只做"监测"定位，不做受 FDA 监管的声明）；规格演进（MHS 是研究预览版，固件开源 + 兼容层）；物理直觉局限（写操作必须过实体批准键——这正是按键存在的理由）；进度（硬件会延期，对策是范围纪律 + 月度构建日志）；商标与许可证（OpenWorker 为 MIT 开源项目 © 2024 Andrew Ng，本 Deck 为社区配套项目，与 Andrew Ng、deeplearning.ai 及 OpenWorker 官方无隶属或背书关系，名称仅用于说明兼容性；独立声明置于首屏、README 顶部与页脚）；声明纪律（检测场景为设计目标而非保证；诊所场景只描述维护记录存储、绝不涉及患者数据；安全信号是"提请人工复核"而非"防止"；页面价格为众筹价，未来零售价不受本众筹锁定）。

// 12 · faq

FAQ — 六问，覆盖全部疑虑

English

1. Does it need the cloud? No. The local model runs sensing, anomaly detection, risk prediction and storage — private data is computed on the Deck and never uploaded by default. The fleet bridge is opt-in and labeled.

2. What happens if I pull the cartridge out? Nothing breaks. Compute keeps running, buffering to 8 GB eMMC. Your history travels with the cartridge — hardware-encrypted, key inside it. Re-insert and the buffer syncs into the archive.

3. How is my personal data kept private? Three layers: local-model compute, encrypted removable storage, offline-by-default network.

4. Can it control equipment, or only watch? Both, through MHS — reads are always safe; writes require explicit approval on the physical buttons by default.

5. Which agents does it work with? Any MHS-compatible agent — OpenWorker and Claude today; the standard is model-agnostic. MQTT-client agents subscribe to the topic tree too.

6. Why not just buy an industrial gateway from Moxa or USR IOT? They solve a different problem — protocol translation for SCADA and clouds. The Deck covers the same field protocols and the MQTT message layer plus MHS-native registration an agent can operate, the approval console, sensing, local-model detection, the vault cartridge and risk prediction. Not a cheaper gateway — a different category: the gateway your agent operates.

中文

1. 需要云吗？不需要。本地模型跑感知、异常检测、风险预测与存储——隐私数据在 Deck 上计算，默认从不上传。车队云桥接可选且明确标注。

2. 拔出存储卡会怎样？什么都不坏。计算继续，缓存在 eMMC。历史跟着卡走——硬件加密，密钥在卡里。重新插入，缓冲自动归档。

3. 隐私怎么保障？三层：本地模型计算 · 可拔加密存储 · 默认离线。

4. 能控制设备还是只能看？都能，走 MHS——读永远安全；写必须过实体按键的明确批准。

5. 兼容哪些 Agent？任何 MHS 兼容 Agent——今天的 OpenWorker 和 Claude；标准与模型无关。

6. 为什么不直接买 Moxa 或有人的网关？它们解决的是另一个问题——为 SCADA 和云做协议翻译。Deck 覆盖同样的现场协议，外加 Agent 可操作的 MHS 注册、批准控制台、四模态感知、本地模型、隐私卡与风险预测。不是更便宜的网关——是不同品类：Agent 能亲手操作的网关。
