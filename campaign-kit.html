开篇故事 — 三段式：没有手 → 时机 → 缺的那只手

English

Your AI colleague is brilliant — and completely handless.

It reads your code, browses your docs, writes your tests. But walk it into a workshop, a clinic, a lab, or a barn, and it goes blind: it can't feel the bearing wear in your compressor, can't read the sterilizer's cycle counter, can't tell you the tractor threw a code at 3 a.m.

Anthropic's new Model Hardware Standard just gave agents a standard way to operate physical devices — standard drivers, plain-language safety labels, safe read/write calls. The first adopters are top labs and advanced manufacturers, where Claude now tunes instruments overnight.

What's still missing is the box on your side: something that speaks fieldbus to your equipment and MHS to your agent, watches the night shift, keeps your data physically yours, and puts permission on physical buttons you can actually press.

That's the Deck. One device, six jobs — a desk your AI coworker sits at.

中文

你的 AI 同事很聪明 —— 但它没有手。

它读你的代码、翻你的文档、写你的测试。可一旦走进车间、诊所、实验室或谷仓，它就瞎了：它摸不到空压机轴承的磨损，读不到灭菌器的循环计数，更不知道拖拉机凌晨三点报了码。

Anthropic 新发布的模型硬件标准（MHS）刚给 Agent 开了一条操作物理设备的标准通道——标准化驱动、自然语言安全标签、安全的读写指令。首批接入的是顶级实验室和先进制造商：Claude 已经能在那里通宵调仪器了。

还缺的是你这一侧的那只手：一台对下说现场总线、对上说 MHS 的设备；一台替你值守夜班、把数据物理地留在你手里、把权限放在真实按键上的设备。

这就是 Deck。一台设备，六件事 —— 你的 AI 同事坐镇的桌面。

// 05 · privacy · 核心差异节

隐私架构 — 本项目的灵魂卖点

English

The floppy's form. The vault's soul.

The Deck's archive lives on a removable, hardware-encrypted cartridge — up to 512 GB. The key lives in the cartridge, not the machine. Pull it out and your history physically leaves with you: nothing readable stays behind.

And your private data is computed by a local model, on-device. Telemetry, logs, histories — inference happens where the data lives, so understanding your data never requires uploading it.

Compute and storage are cleanly split:

compute plane:  local model · NPU anomaly detection · MHS registry · protocol bridge   (always on)
storage plane:  hardware-encrypted cartridge · 8 GB eMMC buffer                         (swappable · yours)
private data:   computed by the local model, on the Deck                                (never uploaded by default)
cartridge out:  compute keeps running → eMMC buffer · history rides in your pocket

What the split buys you:

  • Privacy by physics — pull the cartridge and it's yours; no remote wipe needed, no "trust us"
  • Local-model privacy — personal data computed on the Deck itself
  • Independent upgrade — swap cartridge capacity without touching compute
  • Failure isolation — a dead cartridge doesn't kill the sensor
  • Audit path — hand the auditor the cartridge, not the machine
中文

软盘的形态，保险库的内核。

Deck 的档案库装在一张可拔的硬件加密存储卡里——最高 512GB。密钥在卡里，不在机器里。拔出来，你的历史就物理地跟你走了：机器里不留任何可读内容。

你的隐私数据由本地模型计算，就在设备上。遥测、日志、历史——推理发生在数据所在之处，理解你的数据从不需要上传它。

计算与存储，干净分离：拔卡不停机——计算继续，缓存在 eMMC；换卡不碰固件；卡坏不影响传感；审计交卡不交机器。

// 06 · risk prediction

风险预测 — 从"预测维护"升级为"三类风险"

English

Risk, briefed — not buried.

Most "predictive maintenance" boxes predict one thing: failure. Real equipment carries three risks, and the Deck briefs all of them, every morning, per fleet:

  • Failure risk — RUL curves: bearing wear, belt stretch, filter clog, refrigerant loss. Alerts days or weeks before, never after.
  • Safety risk — CO trends, thermal-runaway precursors, overload patterns. Escalated before danger, not logged after it.
  • Compliance risk — inspection deadlines, sensor-expiry dates. The boring one that costs the most when missed.

Each brief carries four fields: likelihood · time window · recommended action · evidence. The agent doesn't just watch — it reads the brief and proposes the next step. You approve it on the physical button.

中文

风险要简报，不是埋起来。多数"预测维护"盒子只会预测一件事：故障。真实世界的设备背着三类风险，Deck 每天早上按车队逐台简报：故障风险（RUL 曲线：轴承磨损、皮带拉伸、滤芯堵塞、制冷剂流失）· 安全风险（CO 趋势、热失控前兆、过载模式——危险之前升级预警）· 合规风险（年检与传感器到期日程——最枯燥、误期最贵的那类）。

每份简报四要素：可能性 · 时间窗 · 建议动作 · 证据。Agent 不只是看——它读简报、提方案，你在实体按键上批准。

配图建议：用落地页模拟屏截图（RISK brief · failure 4% · compliance due 12d）——已实现的 UI 比抽象插画可信。本节向医疗/诊所档位（$899）输送价值。

主文案 — 产品故事

English · body copy

The last time your data had a shape you could hold, it lived on a floppy. You could feel its weight in your pocket, and when you left the office, it left with you. Then the cloud ate that. Your files became someone else's uptime.

The Privacy Cartridge gives the shape back: a 3.5″ floppy silhouette in armored metal — metal shutter, engraved lock icon, a violet status edge that breathes when the disk is healthy. Inside, up to 512 GB of hardware-encrypted storage. The key lives in the disk, not in the machine. Insert it in any Deck and the archive unlocks; pull it out and the slot is just an empty slot holding nothing.

Compute here. Store there. The Deck runs two planes, and they're separated on purpose. The compute plane — NPU, local model, sensing — never leaves the device. The storage plane is the cartridge. Pull it and the archive physically leaves with you; the Deck keeps running on 8 GB of internal buffer and syncs the moment you re-insert. Nothing breaks. Nothing leaks. That's what compute-storage separation means when it's made of metal instead of policy documents.

Pull-to-own: three seconds, no menus. No settings screen, no export wizard, no "preparing your archive" progress bar. You eject your data the way you ejected floppies — by hand. Your AI coworker's entire memory of your workshop fits in a shirt pocket.

中文 · 主文案

你的数据最后一次有形状、拿得起来，是它还在软盘里的时候。你能摸到它的重量，你下班走人，它跟你走。后来云把它吃掉了——你的文件变成了别人机房的在线率。

隐私卡把这个形状还给你：3.5″ 软盘轮廓、装甲金属内核——金属滑盖、铭刻锁孔、一条健康时缓慢呼吸的紫色状态边。里面是最大 512 GB 的硬件加密存储。密钥在盘里，不在机器里。插进任何一台 Deck，档案解锁；拔出来，卡槽就只是一个空槽，什么都留不下。

计算在这，存储在那。Deck 是两个平面，刻意分开。计算平面——NPU、本地模型、传感——永不离机。存储平面就是这张卡。拔卡，档案物理地跟你走；Deck 继续跑在 8 GB 内置缓冲上，回插瞬间同步。什么都不坏，什么都不漏。这就是"计算存储分离"做成金属而不是做成隐私政策的样子。

拔盘即拥有：三秒，无菜单。没有设置页，没有导出向导，没有"正在准备您的档案"进度条。你像当年退软盘一样，用手把数据退出来。你的 AI 同事对你车间的全部记忆，装得进衬衫口袋。
